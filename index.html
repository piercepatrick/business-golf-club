FAQs

How much does membership cost?

For founding members in Scottsdale, Business Golf Club will be $39/mo. This price will rise soon. Already a member? Manage your membership.

When and where are rounds held?

Rounds are at public courses in your city, typically weekends. Tell us when you like to play, your budget, and how far you’re willing to travel, and we’ll handle the rest. You’ll get the course and tee time before you play.

How do you match a group?

We group people based on business and personal factors shared in the personality quiz during sign up. Our algorithm is designed to group you with people that are different enough to keep it interesting, but similar enough to you to make sure you get along well.

What if I can’t make a round?

Before each round, we’ll reach out to see if you’re available and interested in playing, so you can always sit one out. Once you’ve confirmed a tee time, we ask that you let us know at least 48 hours in advance if your plans change. A big part of Business Golf Club is meeting great people and having a full group to play with, so giving us notice helps us fill your spot and ensures a better experience for everyone.

What cities are you in?

As of October 2026 we are only in Scottsdale, Arizona. We plan to expand to more areas throughout 2026 and into 2027.

A note from our founder

I’ve played more than 60 rounds of golf this year, but probably 95% of them have been by myself.

I love golf, so I don’t mind playing alone. But it’s so much better when you’re out there with a good group of people.

With the rise of remote work and the incredibly convenient world we live in, it’s easier than ever to go literal days or weeks without having a meaningful conversation with another person. There have been days where I’ve looked up from my computer and realized I hadn’t talked to a human that wasn’t my wife in far too long.

At the same time, meeting new people as an adult isn’t exactly easy, especially people you actually have something in common with. That’s one thing I do miss about school.

That’s what got me thinking about golf differently.

A round of golf might be one of the best ways to build a real relationship. You spend several hours together, outside, away from a screen. There’s plenty of time to talk, but there’s also something to do, so it never feels like forced networking.

That’s why I started Business Golf Club.

The goal is simple: bring interesting and successful people together through golf.

We’ll organize the rounds. You show up, play golf, and meet people you probably wouldn’t have met otherwise.

If Business Golf Club leads to a few more great rounds or a few new friendships, or a few opportunities that never would have happened otherwise, I think it will be worth it.

- Pierce Patrick
