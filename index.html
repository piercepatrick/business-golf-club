2. We match you with a small group of members in your city.

3. We book a public tee time and send the course, time, and who you’re playing with.

4. You show up and play. That’s the whole product.

Why should you join Business Golf Club?

A good round depends on who is in the other three spots. Most people either play with the same three friends or get dropped into a random pairing. This is the middle: a matched group, a real tee time, and no club initiation. Business can come up. It doesn’t have to. The point is a round you’ll want to play again.

FAQs

How much does membership cost?

Membership is [$X] a month. Green fees and drinks are paid at the course. You can cancel anytime by emailing hello@businessgolf.club. Update this price before you publish.

When and where are rounds held?

Rounds are at public courses in your city, typically weekend mornings. You’ll get the course and tee time before you play.

How do you match a group?

The signup survey covers handicap, pace, and what you want from the round. Groups are close enough to enjoy the golf and different enough to keep it interesting.

Do I need a low handicap?

No. You need to be comfortable playing 18 with people you haven’t met and keeping the group moving. Beginners who are still learning course etiquette may want to wait.

What if I can’t make a round?

Tell us at least 24 hours ahead. Each group is small, so a late cancellation changes the round for everyone else. We’ll check before each round so you can opt out early.

What cities are you in?

We’re opening in [your cities]. If we aren’t in yours yet, join the list and we’ll write when a tee sheet opens. To suggest a city, email hello@businessgolf.club.

A note from our founder

I wanted a regular game with people I hadn’t already met through work. The usual options were a private club, a random pairing, or a group chat that never turned into a tee time.

The club version was more commitment than I wanted. The random pairing was a coin flip. The group chat stayed a group chat.

Business Golf Club is the simple version: we ask if you’re free, match a small group, book a public tee time, and get out of the way.

Some rounds will turn into a standing game. Some will turn into a deal, a hire, or a friend. Some will just be a good eighteen. All of those are a successful round.
